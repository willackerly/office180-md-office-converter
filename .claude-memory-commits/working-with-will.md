description: How to interpret autonomy and completion requests
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Once Will approves a direction, continue autonomously through in-scope
implementation, proportional verification, documentation consistency, and a
commit-ready ground state. Ask only at a genuine authority boundary or dead
end. Enthusiastic approval authorizes the agreed implementation and tests; it
does not silently authorize pushing, publishing, deployment, or exposing
private artifacts. Evidence: repeated Office180 project sessions through
2026-08-12.
